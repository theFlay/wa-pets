How the licence works

Category A: no licence

Common aviary birds (budgies, cockatiels, zebra finches, galahs...) plus all invertebrates that aren't threatened. No frogs or reptiles are exempt.

Category B: standard licence

  • 18+ and a permanent resident
  • No wildlife convictions
  • Show some husbandry knowledge
  • Secure enclosure
  • Apply 20 business days before getting the animal

Category C: advanced licence

  • Have held Category B for 12+ months with no issues
  • Written OK from your local council
  • At least one written reference
  • Locked enclosures
  • Dangerous species: home inspection, warning signs, first aid kit, compression bandages, snake gear

Species list and categories: DBCA "Fauna possessing (pet keeper's) licence" guidelines, Appendix 1 (Biodiversity Conservation Regulations 2018). DBCA was reviewing the list in late 2025, so species may be added or moved. Check the current list with DBCA (wildlifelicensing@dbca.wa.gov.au, 08 9219 9000) before you buy.

Size, care and temperament notes are general hobby guidance, not DBCA's. Care level is Claude's rough judgement. "Around Perth/Peel" means the species lives wild in the area. You still can't take animals from the wild; buy captive-bred from licensed keepers.
