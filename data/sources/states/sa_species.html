Native animal keeping categories

Native animals are grouped into 4 keeping categories:

  • Basic
  • Specialist
  • Exempt, and
  • Unprotected.

Basic, Specialist and Exempt category animals are all considered ‘protected animals’ under the National Parks and Wildlife Act 1972. Protected animals are any native mammal, bird or reptile, or any amphibian listed as a threatened species.

To find out the category your native animal is in, please download the species list (PDF) or check the full list below.

If you can’t find your protected native animal on the species list that means it is a Specialist category animal. Specialist category animals are not on the list because there are thousands of them. A Specialist animal is any protected animal not on the below species list.

For more information on applying for permits see Keep and sell native animal or egg permits.

Acknowledgement of Country

The state government acknowledges Aboriginal people as the First Peoples and Nations of the lands and waters we live and work upon and we pay our respects to their Elders past, present and emerging. We acknowledge and respect the deep spiritual connection and the relationship that Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people have to Country.

We work in partnership with the First Peoples of South Australia and support their Nations to take a leading role in caring for their Country.
