Australian Capital Territory

Nature Conservation (Exempt Animals)
Declaration 2026

Disallowable instrument DI2026-61
made under the

Nature Conservation Act 2014, s 155 (Declarations—exempt animals)

1 Name of instrument
         This instrument is the Nature Conservation (Exempt Animals) Declaration
         2026.

2 Commencement
         This instrument commences on the day after its notification day.

3 Declaration
         I declare the species listed in schedule 1 to be exempt animals.

4 Revocation
         This instrument revokes the Nature Conservation (Exempt Animals)
         Declaration 2019 (DI2019-66).

Bren Burkevics
Conservator of Flora and Fauna
11 May 2026
