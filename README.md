# WA pet keeper's licence species guide

A browsable guide to all 156 native animals the WA Department of Biodiversity, Conservation and Attractions (DBCA) approves for pet keeping under the Fauna possessing (pet keeper's) licence: frogs, reptiles and birds, grouped by licence category (A exempt, B standard, C advanced).

- Species list and categories: DBCA pet keeper's licence guidelines, Appendix 1 (Biodiversity Conservation Regulations 2018). Check the current list with DBCA before buying.
- Care notes are general hobby guidance.
- Photos: Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons contributors under Creative Commons licences. Each card links to its photo's credit page.
